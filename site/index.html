Method & limits

Demand

Every model from the watchlist with an audio pipeline tag, created in the last 12 months. Each model adds 1 + log10(1 + downloads) to each language it's tagged with, divided by the square root of its language count so a 100-language checkpoint doesn't swamp targeted releases. Speech recognition demand includes automatic-speech-recognition and audio-text-to-text (audio LLMs); synthesis demand is text-to-speech. Downloads are Hugging Face's trailing 30-day count.

Supply

Every dataset tagged task_categories:automatic-speech-recognition or text-to-speech with a commercially usable licence (CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, Apache-2.0, MIT, ODC-BY, PDDL, BSD), weighted by its size category from 1 (under 1K rows) to 7 (100M–1B rows). Sizes above 1B rows are nearly always wrong tags, so they count as 1. Non-commercial, no-derivatives, research-only, "other" and missing licences are excluded. Datasets with fewer than 10 downloads and no likes are treated as placeholders and skipped.

Gap score

100 × demand ÷ (1 + supply). The top 10 only includes pairs with releases from at least two watched labs.

Limits

  • Open releases only. Closed labs show up only through what they publish openly. OpenAI’s last open audio model on Hugging Face is Whisper large-v3-turbo (Oct 2024), and closed-only providers such as ElevenLabs don’t appear at all.
  • Licence and size tags are self-reported by uploaders and are sometimes wrong. A CC-BY tag on a dataset of scraped audio doesn't make it clean to train on.
  • Language tags are optional. Many Meta and ESPnet releases carry none, so they count in the lab briefs but not the grid.
  • Row counts aren't hours. Size categories count rows, not audio duration or speaker diversity.

Pipeline

A GitHub Action runs fetch.py every day, writes data.json and a dated snapshot, and redeploys this page. The weekly diff compares today against the snapshot closest to seven days ago. Source on GitHub.
